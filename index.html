Formulas & steps

Step 1 — Water excess/deficit by Na
Na normal = 140 ± 2 mEq/L
Low Na → relative water excess, acidifying effect
High Na → relative water deficit, alkalinizing effect

Step 2 — Chloride excess/deficit
Corrected Cl = measured Cl × 140 / measured Na
Cl normal ≈ 102 mEq/L
Corrected Cl > 102 → chloride excess, acidifying effect
Corrected Cl < 102 → chloride deficit, alkalinizing effect

Step 3 — Effective SID
HCO₃ = 0.0301 × PaCO₂ × 10^(pH − 6.1)
Albuminate⁻ = 0.25 × Albumin(g/L)
Pi⁻ = 0.6 × Phosphate(mg/dL)
eSID / SIDe = HCO₃ + Albuminate⁻ + Pi⁻; normal ≈ 39 ± 1 mEq/L

Step 4 — Unmeasured strong anions
Ca charge = Ca(mg/dL) × 0.5 mEq/L
Mg charge = Mg(mEq/L)
UA / SIG = Na + K + Ca charge + Mg charge − Cl − eSID
Normal UA/SIG ≈ 8 ± 2 mEq/L
Lactate is not subtracted from SIDa; it is included in UA/SIG with keto-acids, sulfate, and other organic anions.

Clinical meaning
Na 反映 free water effect；corrected Cl 用來避免 dilution/concentration 誤判 chloride excess/deficit；eSID 反映 bicarbonate、albumin、phosphate 所解釋的 effective charge balance；UA/SIG 代表 Cl 以外的 unmeasured strong anions，包含 lactate、keto-acids、sulfate 與其他 organic anions。
